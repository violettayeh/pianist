About關於

Where music
meets movement

Violetta began piano at the age of five and was selected for Taiwan’s Music Gifted and Talented Education Programme at eight. She holds a B.M. in Piano Performance and a B.A.

五歲開始學習鋼琴，八歲入選臺灣音樂資優教育班，奠定古典鋼琴演奏基礎。擁有音樂學士（鋼琴演奏）及文學士學位。

Her work includes collaborations with singers, instrumentalists and dancers, ballet accompaniment, and original music for interdisciplinary projects involving visual art. Her performances and collaborations have taken her to Taipei, Sicily, Los Angeles, Prague and London.

她的藝術實踐涵蓋合作演奏、芭蕾伴奏及跨領域創作，合作對象包括歌者、器樂演奏家與舞者，亦曾為視覺藝術計畫創作原創音樂。演出與合作足跡遍及臺北、西西里、洛杉磯、布拉格及倫敦。

With over 11 years of teaching experience, she places listening, communication and artistic expression at the heart of her practice.

擁有十一年以上音樂教學經驗，重視聆聽、溝通與藝術表達，並將其融入教學及合作實踐。

Based in London and Paris, she welcomes enquiries for teaching, performances and artistic collaborations.

現居倫敦與巴黎，歡迎教學、演出及藝術合作邀約。

Artistic Journey藝術歷程

In my own words我的藝術歷程

2023Sicily · Italy

In 2023, I participated in the Sicily International Piano & Vocal Festival in Italy as a solo pianist, where the masterclasses and vocal collaboration deepened my understanding of accompaniment.

2023 年，我以獨奏鋼琴家的身分參加義大利西西里國際鋼琴及聲樂音樂節，大師班與聲樂合作讓我對伴奏有了更深的理解。

2024–25Taipei · Prague

In 2024, I was selected for collaborative teacher training by the Formosa Ballet Education and Music Collaboration Association, participating in workshops and repertoire coaching. I attended the Czech National Ballet Summer Workshop in 2025 and worked as a ballet pianist at Gravity Ballet in Taipei.

2024 年，我獲福爾摩沙芭蕾教育與音樂合作協會選入合作教師培訓，參與工作坊與曲目指導。2025 年參加捷克國家芭蕾暑期工作坊，並於台北 Gravity Ballet 擔任芭蕾鋼琴家。

2026Autumn · London

This autumn, I collaborated with artist Tere Chad, other artists and a contemporary composer to create original music for Threading Organisms 3.0 at London’s Bloomsbury Festival. The project combined textile art, dance and performance with icandance, a charity supporting disabled children and young people through creative expression. It demonstrated how music fosters connection across art forms.

今年秋天，我與藝術家 Tere Chad、其他藝術家及一位當代作曲家合作，為倫敦 Bloomsbury Festival 的《Threading Organisms 3.0》創作原創音樂。該計畫結合紡織藝術、舞蹈與表演，並與 icandance 合作——這是一個透過創意表達支持身心障礙兒童及青少年的慈善機構。這個計畫讓我看見音樂如何促進不同藝術形式之間的連結。
